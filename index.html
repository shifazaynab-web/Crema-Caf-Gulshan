Our Story

A Chic, Open‑Air Corner of Gulshan

Crema Café Gulshan was built around one simple idea: coffee and food should feel effortless, unfussy, and genuinely good. Every drink is layered and finished by hand — no shortcuts, no filler.

Tucked into Road 104 opposite the German Club, the space is designed to feel airy and calm — a spot to slow down with a specialty latte, catch up over a rice bowl, or simply watch Gulshan go by.
